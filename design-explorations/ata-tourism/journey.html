I · The highland capital

Asmara, a city drawn on a grid

Asmara sits on Eritrea's central plateau at about 2,325 m. Its historic centre was laid out between 1893 and 1941 and is inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List (2017) as Asmara: A Modernist African City.

  • Fiat TaglieroA 1938 service station by Giuseppe Pettazzi: a reinforced-concrete aeroplane with outstretched wings.
  • Cinema ImperoA 1937 Art Deco cinema by Mario Messina on Harnet Avenue.
  • Harnet AvenueThe main avenue and spine of the historic centre.
  • MedeberA working quarter turning scrap metal into goods, visited with respect.

II · The escarpment

About 2,300 m of descent

Between Asmara and Massawa the road falls from plateau to sea level in switchbacks. Highland terraces give way to dry slopes, then to the coastal plain and the salt light of the sea.

Keep scrolling: the map follows the road down and the altimeter falls with you. It is a long seated drive with continuous curves, and ATA will talk you through the day.

A cableway built in 1937 once linked the two cities. It no longer operates.

III · The Red Sea port

Massawa, built from the reef

The old town stands on a coral island. Ottoman, Egyptian and Italian builders each left their mark, much of it cut from reef coral. The independence war damaged it, and parts of the old town remain in ruins, including the Imperial Palace built in the 1870s.

IV · Two climates

Two rain calendars, one journey

The highlands and the coast run on different seasons. Pick a month to see which side is wetter, then ask us what that month looks like on the ground.

Climate patterns, not forecasts. Highland weather stays mild all year; the coast is hot in mid-summer.
