# Policy and Professional-Review Register — Claude WP-04 v0.1

**Status:** Register of items requiring qualified professional review. **No legal, medical or safety conclusion is offered here.** Identifying that review is needed is the deliverable; performing it is not Claude's or YK Systems' role.

---

## 1. Why this register exists separately

Some content cannot be made safe by careful writing. Cancellation terms, refund rules, health guidance and water-activity safety carry consequences that need someone professionally accountable. Drafting plausible-sounding wording for these would be the most dangerous thing this project could produce — it would look finished and carry no authority.

So they are listed, routed, and left blocked.
