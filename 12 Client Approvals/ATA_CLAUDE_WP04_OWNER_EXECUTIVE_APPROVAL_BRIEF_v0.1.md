## 3. What has been verified

Independent research established facts that **do not need your confirmation** — they are externally true and now documented with sources:

- Asmara sits at about **2,325 m**; Massawa at sea level. The drive between them descends roughly **2,300 m**.
- Eritrea has **three rainy seasons**, not one. Asmara and Massawa are on **opposite rainfall calendars**.
- Massawa averages roughly **41 °C** daytime highs in July.
- Asmara's historic centre is on the **UNESCO World Heritage List** (2017) — as a *planned city*, not building-by-building.
- Fiat Tagliero (1938, Giuseppe Pettazzi) and Cinema Impero (1937, Mario Messina) are correctly dated and attributed.
- Massawa's old town is coral-stone, Ottoman/Egyptian/Italian layered, and **partly war-damaged**.

That is a real asset. It means your site can say substantial, accurate things about Eritrea before you have settled a single commercial detail.

## 4. What remains unverified

**Everything commercial.** Not because research failed, but because these facts exist only inside ATA:

Prices · departure dates · availability · capacity · accommodation · transport · inclusions and exclusions · cancellation and refund terms · payment handling · whether specific sites are actually visited · whether the boat excursion runs · realistic journey duration · your response time · your licences · testimonials.

One research finding needs your urgent attention: **multiple sources state a travel permit is required to travel outside Asmara.** If accurate, every traveller on your flagship needs one for the Massawa leg. We could not confirm this against an official source and have published nothing about it. It may affect how the tour operates.

## 5. What you control

**All business facts.** Price, dates, capacity, what is included, your policies, and — critically — **every customer confirmation**. No system, and no one at YK Systems, can confirm a booking on your behalf. When a customer is told "confirmed", that came from you.

You also control **publication**: content does not go live because it was written. It goes live because you released it.

## 6. What YK Systems controls

The platform: schema, environments, access mechanics, deployment, backups, the audit trail. We administer the system and support you in using it.

**We are deliberately not able to approve your business facts.** This is enforced in the software, not just promised — the technical administrator role has no content-approval permission. If we could both change the system and certify the content, the record would be worth less.

## 7. What the dashboard will let you change

Tour title and summary · destinations · price and pricing type · currency · dates and date status · capacity and availability · itinerary · inclusions and exclusions · accommodation · transport · cancellation and refund policy · payment information · contact details · images and media rights · destination descriptions · climate information · operational warnings · booking-request instructions · quote and confirmation language.

## 11. The one thing worth saying plainly

You have no reviews, no ratings and no long track record to lean on yet. The research surfaced something useful about that: **candour is your strongest asset.** Telling a traveller honestly that Massawa's old town is partly war-damaged, that July on the coast is genuinely hot, and that a person will confirm their booking personally, will convert better with a diaspora audience than polished claims you cannot support.

The system has been built to make that posture easy and the alternative difficult.
