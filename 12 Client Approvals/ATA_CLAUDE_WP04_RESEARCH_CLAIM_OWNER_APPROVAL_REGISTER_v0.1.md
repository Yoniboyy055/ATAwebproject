## 1. The rule this register enforces

> **APPROVED SOURCE FACT remains zero** unless an authorised owner or reviewer explicitly approves a claim through the defined process.

Research raises the *evidence* level. Only ATA raises the *approval* level. A claim being well-sourced, corroborated and high-confidence still does not make it publishable — it makes it **ready to be considered**.

**Legend — Source status:** `CORROBORATED` multiple independent sources agree · `SINGLE` one source · `CONFLICT` sources disagree · `NONE` no source found
**Legend — Publication:** `READY` awaiting only ATA wording sign-off · `COND` conditional on the stated requirement · `BLOCKED` cannot publish

## 6. TR-01 — the one that needs a decision, not just approval

Multiple travel sources state that a **permit is required to travel outside Asmara**, that tourists may not use public transport outside Asmara, and that permits are sometimes refused. We could not confirm this against any official government source.

If accurate, **every traveller on the flagship needs a permit for the Massawa leg** — the core of the product.

This is not a wording question. Three things must happen, in order:

1. **Verify** against an official source (Eritrean Ministry of Tourism; a national travel advisory).
2. **Decide** whether ATA obtains permits on the traveller's behalf.
3. **Then** decide what the website says — including whether "we handle the paperwork" is a claim ATA can truthfully make.

Nothing about permits may be published before step 1.

## 7. Approval process

For each claim ATA wishes to publish:

1. Read the proposed wording.
2. Confirm it is accurate as far as ATA knows, and acceptable in ATA's voice.
3. Note any limitation that must travel with it.
4. Record the approver's name and date.
5. Set a review date.
6. **Release** as a separate act.

Only after step 6 does a claim become an approved source fact.
