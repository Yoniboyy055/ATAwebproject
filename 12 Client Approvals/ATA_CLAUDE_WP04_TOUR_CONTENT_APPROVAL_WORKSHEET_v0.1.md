| T-07 | Asmara description | "Eritrea's highland capital at about 2,325 m — a planned modernist city whose historic centre UNESCO inscribed on the World Heritage List in 2017." | ☐ **Conditional on UNESCO check** |
| T-08 | Route / overview | "Between Asmara and Massawa the road falls about 2,300 metres — from a highland plateau to sea level on the Red Sea." | ☐ |
| T-09 | Massawa condition disclosure | "Much of it still carries the damage of the independence war, and some of it stands as ruins. It is not a restored heritage quarter; it is a working port town with a long memory." | ☐ **Requires ATA ground-truth confirmation** |

**On T-09.** Research recommends naming the war damage plainly rather than marketing "historic charm". A diaspora traveller who arrives expecting a restored quarter and finds ruins will feel misled; one told honestly often finds it the most affecting part of the trip. **Only ATA can confirm this matches current conditions.** Review in 12 months — condition claims decay.
