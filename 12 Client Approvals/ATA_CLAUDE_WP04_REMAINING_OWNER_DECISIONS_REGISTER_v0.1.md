## 6. The shortest path to a publishable page

You do not need all 41. To publish something honest and useful:

1. **A-01** confirm the flagship.
2. **A-02** choose the pricing model — *quote-only is a valid answer and unblocks immediately*.
3. **A-08** name the approver and releaser.
4. **A-09** set the response commitment.
5. **A-11** supply images with rights.
6. **A-14** privacy notice, for the enquiry form.
7. **R-24** the five-minute UNESCO check.

That combination gives a page with verified destination content, honest seasonal information, working placeholders, a functioning enquiry route, and no unsupported claims. The remaining 34 decisions then improve it incrementally rather than gating it.
