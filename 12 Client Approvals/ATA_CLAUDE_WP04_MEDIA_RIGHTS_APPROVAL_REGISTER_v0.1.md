## 1. The rule

> **No rights record, no publication.** An unlicensed photograph is a legal exposure, not a design detail.

This applies to every visual asset: photographs, video, maps, illustrations, and any image used in social or Open Graph previews.

## 4. Consent — M-12 to M-14

If a person is identifiable, consent is required. This is both a legal matter and, in this context, an ethical one.

**Medeber Market deserves specific mention.** It is a working artisan quarter and people's livelihood. Photographing traders at work without consent, and then using those images to sell tours, is exploitative regardless of what the law permits in any given jurisdiction. Research flagged this; the register enforces it.

☐ ATA confirms photography etiquette guidance is given to guides and travellers.

## 5. Accuracy — M-15

An image is a factual claim about the product.

| Do not use an image that | Because |
|---|---|
| Shows a restored Massawa if the old town is partly ruined | Contradicts the honest condition disclosure |
| Shows accommodation that is not the accommodation used | Misrepresents the product |
| Shows an activity that is not offered | Implies an inclusion |
| Shows a different country | The legacy content's Ethiopia framing is exactly this error |
| Is heavily edited beyond normal correction | Sets an expectation the place cannot meet |

The Massawa condition disclosure and the photography must agree. If the copy says "some of it stands as ruins" and the photograph shows a pristine quarter, the copy is doing honest work and the image is undoing it.

## 6. Prohibited media

| Never use | Why |
|---|---|
| Images from the reference mockups | Design references only; not licensed |
| Any UNESCO logo or emblem | Restricted; requires permission and professional review |
| Images scraped from other operators | Copyright infringement |
| Stock images presented as ATA's own tours | Misrepresentation |
| Images of identifiable people without consent | Legal and ethical exposure |
| AI-generated images of real places presented as photographs | Fabricated depiction of a real product |
