## How to record a decision

Each row needs four things: **what** was decided, **who** decided it, **when**, and **what it unblocks**. A decision without a named decider is not auditable.

Use one of three outcomes: **Decided** · **Deferred** (with an owner and a date) · **Needs review** (routed to a professional).
