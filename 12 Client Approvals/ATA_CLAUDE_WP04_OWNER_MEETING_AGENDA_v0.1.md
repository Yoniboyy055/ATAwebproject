## 1. Opening — 5 min

Where the project stands: the platform is built; the missing input is ATA's business facts. Approved source facts today: **zero**. That is expected, not a problem.

## 2. Confirm the approved governance decisions — 5 min

Ten decisions already settled (environment, schema replacement, invitation-only auth, payments disabled, later production, ATA owns business facts, YK owns technical, legacy archived and not published).

**Ask:** any objection? If none, they are locked.

## 3. Legacy packages — 10 min

The four records (299 / 599 / 899 / 1499) are Ethiopia-focused, wrong product, unverified. Classified **NON-AUTHORITATIVE LEGACY SEED DATA**.

**Decisions needed:**
- ☐ Confirm the classification
- ☐ Confirm none is a current product
- ☐ Set archive retention period (A-32)
- ☐ Any replacement product wanted? (A-33)

## 4. Roles and authority — 10 min

Six roles exist in code. YK Systems **cannot** approve ATA business facts — enforced in software.

**Decisions needed:**
- ☐ Name the ATA Owner account
- ☐ Name the Administrator(s)
- ☐ Name the Content Manager
- ☐ Name the Operations Manager
- ☐ **Who approves business facts?** (A-08)
- ☐ **Who releases content publicly?** (A-08)
- ☐ **Who may confirm a booking?** (A-08)
- ☐ Name billing / backup / recovery / incident / privacy / deletion owners (A-34–A-39)

## 5. The commercial core — 25 min

The longest item, because it blocks the most.

**Pricing (A-02)** — the key question is not "what is the price" but "will you publish one at all?" Quote-only is legitimate and unblocks immediately.
- ☐ Pricing status and type
- ☐ If publishing: currency, adult/child/group, deposit, taxes, validity

**Dates and capacity (A-21, A-22)**
- ☐ Any confirmed departures? Or dates-to-be-confirmed for now?
- ☐ Minimum and maximum participants
- ☐ Booking cutoff

**Season strategy (A-04)** — Massawa averages roughly **41 °C** in July. Asmara's rains fall June–September. The two-climate frame lets you publish honest seasonal content *without* deciding this today.
- ☐ Summer, cooler window, both, or defer

## 6. Operations and safety — 15 min

**Permits (A-03)** — the most consequential unresolved item. Multiple sources say a permit is needed outside Asmara; unverified officially.
- ☐ Who verifies, by when?
- ☐ Does ATA obtain permits for travellers?

**Itinerary (A-05)** — each site needs confirming as actually visited and enterable.
**Marine activity (A-16)** — does the boat trip run? Under what safety arrangements?
**Journey duration (A-17)** — only ATA can state it.

## 7. Policies and professional review — 10 min

24 items need qualified review: 10 legal, 5 safety, 4 medical, 3 cultural, 2 official verification.

**Decisions needed:**
- ☐ Who is your legal adviser, and when do they start?
- ☐ Will you engage an Eritrean cultural reviewer for historical framing?
- ☐ Accept that policy content stays hidden until reviewed?

## 8. Research approval — 10 min

Eleven claim groups are ready for wording sign-off now. Ten are conditional. Four need professional review. Seven are recommended for rejection.

- ☐ Approve the four immediately-releasable items (destinations, Massawa description, climate frame, 2,300 m descent)
- ☐ Approve the UNESCO wording, if the source check is done
- ☐ Accept the recommended omissions (cathedral date, population, journey duration)

## 9. First release — 5 min

Agree what goes live first. Recommended: the four verified items above, with placeholders elsewhere. This proves the pipeline and gives the site real content.

- ☐ Agree the first release set
- ☐ Agree who performs the release

## 10. Close — 5 min

- ☐ Confirm the decision log is complete
- ☐ Assign owners and dates to every unresolved item
- ☐ Agree the next checkpoint
