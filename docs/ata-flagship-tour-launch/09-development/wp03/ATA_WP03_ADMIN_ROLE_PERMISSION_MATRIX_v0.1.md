# Administrative Role and Permission Matrix v0.1

**Status:** Proposed / staging-only unless stated otherwise  
**Baseline:** `b69b512384740658e318c43d5de36aafe43b092d`

ATA Owner: business approvals, access, manual confirmation. ATA Administrator: delegated administration and operations. ATA Content Manager: draft/edit, never approve. ATA Operations Manager: requests, never publish or manage access. YK Technical Administrator: technical staging controls and audit read, never business approval or confirmation. Read-only Auditor: read-only evidence and audit. Assignment and revocation require a named ATA access owner and audit event.

## Control statement

No production system, customer record, payment, notification, public content, or business-sensitive decision is changed by this document.
