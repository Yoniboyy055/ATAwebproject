# ATA visual review checklist

Open `/ata-review`, then review in this order:

- [ ] Homepage feels recognisably ATA and professionally established.
- [ ] Flagship journey is clear without promising facts ATA has not approved.
- [ ] “Request current pricing” is the right public placeholder.
- [ ] “Dates to be confirmed” is the right public placeholder.
- [ ] Request, quote and payment-pending language do not sound confirmed.
- [ ] Only “Manually confirmed” reads as confirmation.
- [ ] Owner overview makes urgent work easy to see.
- [ ] Tour, price and date editing concepts match how ATA works.
- [ ] Evidence, verification, ATA approval and release are clearly separate.
- [ ] Roles reflect ATA business authority and YK Systems technical authority.
- [ ] Mobile navigation, content and forms are easy to use.
- [ ] ATA can identify the facts, prices, dates, policies and images it must supply next.

This is demonstration data. Nothing reviewed here has been published or written to ATA’s live system.
