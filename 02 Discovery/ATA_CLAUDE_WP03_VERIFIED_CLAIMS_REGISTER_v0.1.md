**TR-01 is the most operationally consequential finding in this package.** If accurate, every traveller on an Asmara–Massawa itinerary requires a permit for the Massawa leg. That bears on ATA's process, on what the site should tell customers, and on the honesty of any "we handle the paperwork" claim. It is raised in the ATA decision register and the risk register. **I did not verify it against an official source and it must not be published on tier-4 sourcing alone.**
