## C. Risks explicitly *not* increased

WP-03 created no new commercial, pricing, availability, capacity, supplier, policy or payment risk, because it determined none of those. It also introduced no repository, database, infrastructure or Codex-architecture risk — no implementation file, schema, migration or configuration was touched.

## D. Net risk posture

The package **reduces publication risk overall**: it removes one banned phrase with a defensible replacement, converts an unresolvable "best time" claim into an honest factual frame, and pre-empts a set of marketing temptations (superlatives, promotional adjectives, understated journey time, concealed ruin condition) that would otherwise have reached a page.

It **raises visibility** of one previously unrecognised operational dependency (V-019 permits) that materially affects the flagship's feasibility as described. Surfacing it now is cheaper than discovering it at launch.
