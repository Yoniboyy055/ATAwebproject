# Prohibited and High-Risk Claims Register — Claude WP-03 v0.1

**Purpose:** a single blocklist for anything WP-03 research established must **not** be said, or must not be said without a named precondition. Intended to extend the WP-02 copy-contract prohibited-claims list.

## A. Prohibited outright — do not publish in any form

| # | Prohibited claim/wording | Why | Category |
|---|---|---|---|
| P-01 | "UNESCO-like architecture" | Inaccurate; understates real status while implying something unverified (V-010) | UNESCO |
| P-02 | "UNESCO-approved", "UNESCO-endorsed", "UNESCO partner", or any UNESCO logo/emblem | UNESCO endorses no commercial operator; emblem use is restricted | UNESCO |
| P-03 | Calling any individual building "UNESCO-listed" (Fiat Tagliero, Cinema Impero, the Cathedral, etc.) | They are contributing elements within the inscribed property, not separate inscriptions | UNESCO |
| P-04 | "All of Asmara is a World Heritage Site" | The property is a bounded historic perimeter (~480 ha), not the municipality | UNESCO |
| P-05 | "UNESCO-certified guides" | No such certification exists | UNESCO |
| P-06 | World Heritage status used to imply safety, service quality or reliability | Inscription is a heritage-protection status only | UNESCO |
| P-07 | Any single "Best time to visit [months]" for the whole tour | Factually unsound across two opposite climate regimes | Climate |
| P-08 | "Perfect weather year-round" / "great climate all year" | False for Massawa in summer (~41 °C July) | Climate |
| P-09 | "Cool sea breezes" as summer mitigation for Massawa | Unsupported; July is least humid and windiest yet still ~41 °C | Climate |
| P-10 | Any specific journey duration for the escarpment drive | Only source was an unreliable algorithmic estimate; operational fact | Journey |
| P-11 | "Hundreds of hairpin bends" as a count | Not a verified figure | Journey |
| P-12 | "Ride the historic cableway" or implying it operates | The 1937 cableway does not operate | Journey |
| P-13 | Cathedral construction date | Unresolved source disagreement | Asmara |
| P-14 | Any specific Asmara population figure | Inter-source variance ~783 k–1.15 m | Asmara |
| P-15 | "Pristine", "untouched", "undiscovered", "hidden gem" | Promotional, unverifiable; at odds with a war-damaged working port | Massawa |
| P-16 | Imperial Palace presented as an intact or visitable palace | It is a ruin | Massawa |
| P-17 | Comparative superlatives — "best-preserved in Africa", "most spectacular drive", "world's finest" **asserted in ATA's voice** | Unverifiable comparative claims. May be *attributed* to a named source, never asserted | All |
| P-18 | "Little Rome" / "a slice of Italy in Africa" | Colonial-nostalgic; erases Eritrean agency; poorly suited to a diaspora audience | Cultural |
| P-19 | Any statement that a named site is open, accessible or included | Operational — ATA only (museum, palace, boat, restaurants) | Operational |
| P-20 | Any water-safety assurance ("safe swimming", "suitable for children") | Safety claim; no authoritative source; requires ATA + professional review | Safety |
| P-21 | "Easy" as a blanket descriptor for the escarpment day, or blanket "All Ages Welcome" | Contradicts a long mountain descent and water activity (V-018) | Safety |
| P-22 | Health, fitness or medical advice about altitude or heat | Requires professional/medical review | Health |
| P-23 | Precise climate decimals presented as what a traveller will experience | These are long-run averages, not forecasts | Climate |
| P-24 | "We handle all permits" or similar | Depends on TR-01 being true *and* on ATA actually doing it — unverified on both counts | Operational |

## B. Conditional — publishable only with the stated precondition

| # | Claim | Precondition |
|---|---|---|
| C-01 | Any UNESCO inscription wording | Human confirmation of exact phrasing at `whc.unesco.org/en/list/1550/` |
| C-02 | The 480 ha / 4,300 buildings figures | Primary-source confirmation, or omit the numbers |
| C-03 | "Africa's first modernist World Heritage site" | Attribute to source; do not assert as UNESCO's wording |
| C-04 | Massawa old-town condition/ruin wording | ATA confirmation of current ground conditions; 12-month review |
| C-05 | Altitude-adjustment note | ATA + professional review; keep non-medical |
| C-06 | Historical narration touching colonisation or the independence war | ATA approval; Eritrean cultural review recommended |
| C-07 | Any seasonal *recommendation* | ATA seasonal-operations decision (V-014) |
| C-08 | Road distance (~111 km) | Hedge ("roughly") or omit; ATA may supply better |
| C-09 | Travel-permit information (TR-01) | Official-source verification **and** ATA process confirmation |
| C-10 | Cathedral name | ATA chooses among three names in circulation |
| C-11 | "Pearl of the Red Sea" | Present as a traditional epithet, attributed — not as objective fact |

## C. Standing rule

Where research and marketing pull in opposite directions, research wins. Every item in list A was found *because* the reference mockup or an obvious marketing instinct pointed that way — the value of this register is that it names those temptations before they reach a page.
